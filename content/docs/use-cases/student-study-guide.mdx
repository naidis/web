# The Ultimate Student Study Guide with Naidis

<img src="/images/docs/student-study-guide-cover.png" alt="Student Study Guide" className="w-full rounded-xl my-6 border border-white/10" />

Modern studying requires juggling PDF textbooks, scattered lecture recordings, online research, and endless memorization. Naidis transforms Obsidian into a centralized, AI-powered study hub.

## Phase 1: Capturing Lecture Material

### YouTube & Lecture Transcripts
Professors often upload lectures to YouTube or university portals. With Naidis:
1. Paste the URL of the lecture.
2. Naidis extracts the **full transcript** with timestamps.
3. You can click on any timestamp to jump to that moment in the lecture, making it easy to cross-reference confusing concepts.

### PDF & Textbook Processing
Say goodbye to rigid PDF readers.
1. Drag and drop your syllabus or chapter PDFs into Obsidian.
2. Use the Naidis **PDF Extraction Command**.
3. Naidis extracts the text, preserving layout and tables perfectly, converting it into searchable Markdown. No more manual retyping.

## Phase 2: AI-Assisted Comprehension

Once your materials are in Obsidian, you can use the Naidis AI Chat feature.

**Instead of Googling "What is the Krebs Cycle?", ask your vault:**
> *"Based on week 3's lecture notes and textbook chapter 4, explain the Krebs Cycle as if I'm 5."*

The AI uses **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** to find the exact definitions your professor used, ensuring your answers are aligned with your specific coursework, not generic web answers.

## Phase 3: Active Recall and Retention

Highlighting textbooks gives a false sense of security. Real learning requires **Spaced Repetition**.

1. As you read your processed notes, use the Naidis command: **Create Flashcard from Selection**.
2. Naidis automatically formats cloze deletions (fill-in-the-blank) or Question/Answer pairs.
3. Every morning, use the Naidis Review queue. The plugin uses validated spaced repetition algorithms to surface cards right before you're about to forget them.

## The Ultimate Exam Prep Workflow

1. **Week 1-10**: Capture lectures via YouTube transcripts and PDFs. Convert key terms to flashcards.
2. **Review Sessions**: Review your Naidis flashcard queue daily (15 mins/day).
3. **Synthesis**: Before the midterm, ask Naidis: *"Generate a study guide summarizing the 5 main themes from the first half of the semester."*
4. **Mock Testing**: Instruct the Naidis AI: *"Act as my professor. Quiz me one question at a time based on my notes."*
