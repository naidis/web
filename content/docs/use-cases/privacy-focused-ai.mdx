# Privacy-Focused AI in Obsidian

<img src="/images/docs/privacy-focused-ai-cover.png" alt="Privacy Focused AI" className="w-full rounded-xl my-6 border border-white/10" />

As our personal knowledge management (PKM) systems grow, they contain increasingly sensitive information: private journals, financial plans, unpublished manuscripts, and confidential business documents.

While cloud-based AI tools are powerful, sending your personal brain to external servers poses a fundamental privacy risk. Naidis solves this by bringing AI directly to your local device.

## The Privacy Problem with Cloud AI

When you use ChatGPT, Claude, or most cloud-connected Obsidian AI plugins:
- Your notes are sent external servers
- Your queries are logged
- Your data may be used for model training
- You lose access when offline
- Service interruptions break your workflow

## The Naidis Solution: 100% Local AI

Naidis is built from the ground up for a **local-first** architecture. 

### Local LLM Integration

Naidis seamlessly connects with popular local AI runners:
1. **Ollama**: The easiest way to run models like Llama 3, Mistral, and Phi-3 locally.
2. **LM Studio**: A powerful GUI for managing and running GGUF models.

When you configure Naidis to use a local endpoint (e.g., `http://localhost:11434`), your text never leaves your computer.

### Local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Naidis doesn't just chat; it uses RAG to search your vault.
- The **embedding model** (which converts text into searchable vectors) runs locally using `naidis-core`.
- The **vector database** is stored locally in your Obsidian vault's `.naidis` folder.
- The **generation step** runs on your local LLM.

## Setting Up Your Private AI 

### Step 1: Install a Local Runner
We recommend **Ollama** for its simplicity. Download and install it from ollama.com.

### Step 2: Download a Model
Open your terminal and run:
```bash
ollama run llama3
```
This downloads and starts the Llama 3 model (an excellent, capable model for note synthesis).

### Step 3: Configure Naidis
In Obsidian, go to Naidis Settings > AI Models:
- Under "Provider", select "Ollama / Custom Local Endpoint".
- Enter `http://localhost:11434/v1` as the base URL.
- Enter your model name (e.g., `llama3`).

### Step 4: Local Embeddings
Naidis uses its Rust core to handle embeddings entirely offline. No setup is required for this step—Naidis handles chunking and vector storage natively and transparently.
